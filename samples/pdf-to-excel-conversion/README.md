# PDF to Excel Conversion — Work Sample

By Waqar Ahmed — Spreadsheet & Data Specialist (7 years) · Portfolio: https://waqarahmedofficial.github.io

## What this sample shows

A 2-page PDF statement of account (fictional supplier Meridian Office Supplies Ltd to Alder & Vine Interiors Ltd, October 2026) converted into a clean, structured CSV: 34 invoice lines with 6 columns (Invoice, Date, Description, Qty, Unit Price, Amount). The converted figures reproduce the PDF exactly: Subtotal $1,882.32, Sales Tax (8%) $150.59, Total Due $2,032.91. The PDF itself is not published here — its complete text layer is included so every figure can be re-checked against the source.

## Files in this folder

- `converted-statement.csv` — the converted table, ready to open in Excel or Google Sheets
- `source-table-text.txt` — the source PDF's full extracted text layer
- `VERIFICATION.md` — the row/column counts and totals comparison against the PDF

## Verification

The CSV was re-checked against the PDF's own text layer: **34 of 34 rows, 6 of 6 columns, and the subtotal, tax and total-due figures agree exactly** (see `VERIFICATION.md`).
