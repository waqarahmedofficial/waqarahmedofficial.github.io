What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • A service label, not a message — “Dog Daycare & Boarding” is the same headline every competitor can run. It gives a dog owner no reason to choose this resort over the kennel down the road.
  • The best facts are missing — your own website leads with a 20-acre K9 campus and a dog pool. The ad mentions neither — the two things most likely to make an owner stop scrolling are invisible.
  • No benefit for the owner — nothing answers the only question that matters: “Why will my dog be happier here?”
  • No offer and no first step — no special to act on and no clear booking step, so there is no reason to book this week rather than someday.
  • Running unchanged since 2019 — the same label-led ad has been live for years, so it reads as set-and-forget.

What the redesign does differently

  • The campus becomes the headline — 20 acres and a pool are the differentiator no nearby kennel can copy; the ad finally says so.
  • Benefit-led text for owners — it sells the dog’s day — running, swimming, playing — not a list of service names.
  • A direct booking step — “Book Now” instead of “Learn more”, with a low-commitment first step (a single daycare day) in the copy.
  • An offer slot, ready — no price was invented; the marked slot takes a current special the moment the business runs one.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
Headline / copy angle“Dog Daycare & Boarding” — a bare service labelA benefit headline built on the campus: “20 Acres to Run. A Pool to Splash In.” Owners book experiences for their dogs, not category names.
The missing factsThe 20-acre campus and dog pool — the website’s own lead selling points — never mentionedBoth facts moved into the headline, the copy and the creative. A differentiator only works if the audience sees it.
OfferNo offer in the ad; no price offer is published on the website eitherNo price invented. Instead, a clearly marked offer slot sits in the redesign, ready for a current special (a first-day or seasonal offer, for example) whenever the business runs one.
Call to action“Learn more”“Book Now”, plus a low-commitment first step in the text — start with one day of daycare. Lowering the first step raises the chance of a first booking.
