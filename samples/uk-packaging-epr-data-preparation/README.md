# UK Packaging EPR Data Preparation — Work Sample

By Waqar Ahmed — Spreadsheet & Data Specialist (7 years) · Portfolio: https://waqarahmedofficial.github.io

## What this sample shows

A Report Packaging Data (RPD) dataset prepared for a fictional UK producer, Woldside Foods Ltd (large producer: £8.4m turnover, 210 t of packaging in 2025). The 15-column RPD-format dataset covers H2 2026: household packaging 67,100 kg and non-household 19,050 kg — 86,150 kg in total (2026 annual total 180,350 kg). It includes the household disposal-fee estimate (£21,070.50 at Year-2 illustrative rates) and the cost of the two red-rated packaging lines (£1,099.50 more than at amber), plus the supplier data-request template used to collect packaging weights and classifications.

## Files in this folder

- `worked-example-dataset.csv` — the 15-column RPD-format dataset (11 data rows)
- `supplier-data-request-template.csv` — the supplier packaging-data request template
- `method-and-sources.md` — the method, thresholds, deadlines and sources
- `validation-checks.txt` — the validation checks and their results
