# UK packaging EPR data preparation — method and sources

Round C, 2026-11-15. Sample pack for a **fictional** UK producer ("Woldside Foods
Ltd"). All client figures in the worked example are invented for demonstration;
only the law, thresholds, deadlines and fee rates come from real sources.

## What the service is

Prepare a producer's packaging data so it can be uploaded to Defra's **Report
Packaging Data (RPD)** service — a 15-column CSV, one row per activity ×
packaging type — plus the supporting work that makes the numbers right:

1. **Scope screen** — turnover + tonnage threshold test (reg. 24) and producer
   class (brand owner / packer-filler / importer / distributor / seller /
   online marketplace / service provider).
2. **Supplier data chase** — written requests (template supplied) + follow-ups
   + gap log until each SKU's packaging is weighed and classified. This is the
   step producers fail at: ~80% of audits end in resubmission (ERP UK, Jul 2026).
3. **RPD-format workbook** — the 15-column file, whole-number kg, household vs
   non-household, class (primary / secondary / shipment / tertiary), nation
   of sale, RAM rating on household lines.
4. **Fee estimate** — household tonnage × PackUK fee by material and RAM band,
   so the client sees the bill before PackUK issues the notice of liability.
5. **RAM improvement note** — which red-rated lines cost extra and what a
   re-design to amber/green would save (red = +20% in 2026/27, rising to
   1.6× in 2027/28 and 2.0× in 2028/29).
6. **Nation data** — 12-monthly split (England / Scotland / Wales / NI) due
   with the 1 April submission.

**NOT included (stated plainly in any pitch):** registration on the client's
behalf is NOT offered (the approved person must hold the RPD account — the
client's director/partner creates it; a compliance scheme can act, a data
preparer cannot sign); no return filing, no PRN buying, no "compliance"
claims, no legal or tax advice, no RAM certification. The deliverable is the
data pack and the fee model; the client (or their scheme) submits it.

## The worked example (fictional)

- **Producer:** "Woldside Foods Ltd" — fictional food & drink brand,
  packer/filler (PF). Turnover 2025: GBP 8,400,000; packaging supplied 2025:
  210 t → **large producer** (both thresholds passed: > GBP 2m and > 50 t).
- **Period:** H2 2026 (1 Jul–31 Dec 2026), submission due **1 April 2027**.
- **H2 2026:** household 67,100 kg (67.1 t), non-household 19,050 kg,
  total 86,150 kg. H1 2026 total 94,200 kg (fictional) → 2026 annual 180,350 kg.
- **Disposal-fee estimate (Year 2 illustrative rates):** GBP 21,070.50 on the
  67.1 t of household packaging.
- **Red-rating cost:** the two red lines (LDPE flexible film 8.6 t, beverage
  cartons 3.1 t) cost GBP 1,099.50 more than at amber. At the 2027/28 1.6× red
  multiplier the same lines would cost GBP 8,864.80 instead of GBP 6,640.00
  (+GBP 2,224.80) — the forward hook for a RAM-improvement project.
- **Nation data (2026 annual household 138,900 kg):** England 108,342 kg
  (78.0%), Scotland 12,501 kg (9.0%), Wales 11,112 kg (8.0%), Northern
  Ireland 6,945 kg (5.0%).

## Sources (primary unless flagged)

- **Law:** Producer Responsibility Obligations (Packaging and Packaging
  Waste) Regulations 2024, SI 2024/1332 (made 11 Dec 2024, in force 1 Jan
  2025) — legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2024/1332/made; reg. 24 thresholds;
  regs 35–38 reporting; reg. 115 offences; Sch. 13 civil sanctions.
- **Amendment:** SI 2025/1369 (made 17 Dec 2025, in force 1 Jan 2026) —
  Producer Responsibility Organisation appointment, closed-loop food-grade
  plastic offset, fibre-based composite redefinition (≤5% plastic = paper/
  board), merger/brand-transfer rules. legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/1369/made.
- **GOV.UK collection:** "Extended producer responsibility for packaging"
  (updated 29 Oct 2026) — large producers report every 6 months + PRNs +
  PackUK disposal fee + 7-year records; small producers renew by 1 April
  and report annually by 1 April. gov.uk/government/collections/
  extended-producer-responsibility-for-packaging (opened 14 Nov 2026).
- **Deadlines:** GOV.UK "Packaging data: check reporting periods and
  submission deadlines" — H2 2026 due 1 Apr 2027; small producers 2026 data
  due 1 Apr 2027; 2025-data resubmission deadline 1 Sep 2026 (resubmission
  fee GBP 807 direct / GBP 512 via scheme).
- **RPD file format:** GOV.UK "Report packaging data: create your reporting
  file" — exactly 15 columns (organisation_id, subsidiary_id,
  organisation_size, submission_period, packaging_activity, packaging_type,
  packaging_class, packaging_material, material_subtype, from_country,
  to_country, weight_kg, units, transitional_packaging, ram_rag_rating);
  weights in whole kg; RAM rating large producers only. Opened 14 Nov 2026.
- **RAM:** GOV.UK "RAM 2027: overview" — RAM v1.1 (Apr 2025) applies to the
  2026 reporting year; RAM 2027 applies to 2027; 8 materials, 4 stages
  (collection, sortation, reprocessing, application), red/amber/green.
- **Year 1 base fees (PackUK, 27 Jun 2025):** plastic GBP 423/t, fibre-based
  composite 461, paper and board 196, glass 192, aluminium 266, steel 259,
  wood 280, other 259.
- **Year 2 illustrative fees (PackUK/Defra, 16 Dec 2025):** amber = base;
  red = 1.2× amber; green = −9% (illustrative). Plastic 415/455/545;
  paper and board 190/210/250; glass 185/205/245; aluminium 245/270/325;
  steel 260/290/345; fibre-based composite 475/525/630; wood 410/450/540;
  other 205/225/270. Red multiplier rises to 1.6× (2027/28) and 2.0×
  (2028/29). **Confirmed Year 2 fees were NOT published as of Oct 2026** —
  they arrive with the notices of liability PackUK intends to issue by end
  of November 2026. Never quote Year 2 rates as final.
- **Registration fees 2026/27:** large GBP 2,842 direct / GBP 1,803 via
  scheme; small GBP 1,303 direct / GBP 696 via scheme; late registration
  GBP 386.
- **Scale (implied, not official):** PackUK/Defra via letsrecycle (4 Dec
  2025) — 484 businesses ≈ 11% of producers double-charged → ~4,400
  producers in the scheme. The Grocer (Jan 2026) — PackUK emailed 2,078
  producers about overdue Year-1 payments. No official Defra headcount
  published; do not quote a precise population.
- **Pain (secondary, named sources):** ERP UK (10 Jul 2026) — "around 80%
  of audits result in a resubmission"; Resource Media (26 Feb 2026) — Year 1
  funding shortfall of "tens of millions of pounds" from high resubmission
  volumes; British Retail Consortium via Punchline-Gloucester — 85% of
  retailers say the admin/compliance burden "increased significantly";
  Belvoir Farm (The Guardian/Gazette Herald, 20 Sep 2024) — EPR cost
  estimated ~GBP 850,000 vs ~GBP 900,000 profit; Reconomy/The Grocer
  (18 Sep 2026) — 61.8% of flexible plastic assessed Year 1 got a RED
  rating (~245,000 t).
- **Competition (search evidence, 15 Nov 2026):** Fiverr — zero standalone
  UK pEPR data-prep gigs (web search, not internal search); Upwork — one
  global generalist (Aynur J., Baku — EPR readiness/templates, USD 75/195/
  395, Europe/Canada/US focus, not UK-specific); Freelancer.com — US CPG
  EPR data project, avg bid USD 1,162, 21 bids (proves buyers hire
  freelancers for this work). Incumbents (Valpak 5,000+ members, Ecosurety,
  Beyondly, Clarity, Ecoveritas, Wastepack) sell scheme membership or
  quote-only consultancy — no standalone data-prep price published for SMEs.
- **Fallback niches checked this round:** Canada Federal Plastics Registry —
  REALITY PASS (live, mandatory, annual 29 Sep deadlines, ECCC Excel
  template; Phases 2/3 delayed); EU SCIP database — HOLD (live since 2021,
  15M+ notifications, but Dec 2025 Commission proposal to abolish new
  notifications; IUCLID dossier step beyond plain spreadsheets).

## Caveats

- All client figures in the sample pack are fictional; the fee estimate uses
  ILLUSTRATIVE Year 2 rates — re-check confirmed rates (due end Nov 2026)
  before any real client work.
- RAM ratings in the example (amber/red/green per line) are illustrative
  assignments, not assessments — a real RAM assessment follows PackUK's
  methodology line by line.
- Nation split (78/9/8/5%) is illustrative; real splits come from the
  client's sales data.
- The ~4,400-producer figure is implied from a percentage, not an official
  count — use "around 4,000+ producers" at most, and say it is implied.
- The "5% of UK turnover" fine figure that circulated in a PackUK email
  (Jan 2026) was an erroneous threat — Defra admitted the error. Do NOT use
  it. Verified penalties: reg. 115 offences (unlimited fine on indictment),
  Sch. 13 fixed monetary penalty GBP 1,000, variable monetary penalties for
  late/missed reporting.
- Upwork/Fiverr counts are search-index evidence, not logged-in exhaustive
  platform totals.
- EPR and the Plastic Packaging Tax are DIFFERENT regimes — a client may owe
  both; never conflate them (PPT was Round C 2026-11-07).
- Northern Ireland: EU PPWR EPR/deposit chapters do not apply in NI; UK
  pEPR does apply UK-wide under SI 2024/1332.
