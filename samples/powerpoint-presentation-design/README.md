## What this sample shows

An 8-slide quarterly sales review deck designed for a fictional business (NorthPeak Home, Q3 2026): title slide, agenda, a KPI slide (revenue $1.284M, 8,000 orders, $160.50 average order value, 41% repeat customers), a monthly revenue bar chart, a category slide with a donut chart and ranked bars, highlights & challenges, Q4 priorities with targets, and a closing slide. The deck's figures are internally consistent: the monthly revenues ($402K + $438K + $444K) sum to the quarter total, the five category amounts sum to the same $1,284,000 and their shares to 100%, and 8,000 orders × $160.50 average order value equals the revenue figure.

**Format note:** this sample is the HTML rendering of the presentation design, so it can be viewed in any browser. Clients receive the finished deck as a .pptx file.
