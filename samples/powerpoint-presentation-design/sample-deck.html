Highlights & Challenges

✓ What went well

  • Autumn collection sold through 82% of stock within five weeks of launch.
  • Repeat-customer revenue reached 41% of the total — a company best.
  • Conversion rate held at 3.8% even as traffic grew 24% year on year.
  • Lighting range grew fastest (+31%), led by the new rechargeable line.

✕ What needs work

  • Returns rate is 2.1% — above the 1.8% target, mostly sizing/dimension queries in Storage.
  • The August warehouse move cost an estimated $19K in delayed-dispatch refunds.
  • Outdoor Living underperformed plan by 9% after a cooler-than-usual September.
  • Marketplace fees rose to 13.2% of marketplace revenue, squeezing margin.
