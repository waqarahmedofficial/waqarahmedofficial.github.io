What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • A label, not a headline — “Mold Removal Case Studies” names a document, not a reason to act. A homeowner who just found mold is not shopping for case studies.
  • No offer in the ad at all — yet your own website offers a free evaluation of the damage, with an estimate. The easiest first step a worried homeowner could take is missing from the ad.
  • No homeowner benefit — nothing speaks to what the reader gets: the problem found, the source fixed, the home made safe.
  • No urgency, no first step — mold spreads; the ad gives the reader nothing to do next and no reason to do it today.
  • It has run unchanged for years — the same label-led creative has been live since May 2020, so it reads as set-and-forget.

What the redesign does differently

  • Your own free evaluation, in the ad — the offer already exists on your website; the redesign puts it where Sarasota homeowners scroll.
  • A headline written to the homeowner — it names the problem they have (“Found mold?”) instead of a document they do not need.
  • Trust facts that are already yours — state-licensed mold remediators and 24/7 emergency service, both published on your site, now working inside the ad.
  • A direct call step — “Call Now” matches how mold emergencies actually start: with a phone call.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
Headline / copy angle“Mold Removal Case Studies” — a bare service labelA problem-led headline: “Found Mold in Your Home?” Homeowners respond to their problem, not to a document title.
OfferNo offer anywhere in the ad, although the website offers a free damage evaluation with an estimateThe business’s own published Free Evaluation, placed in the ad. The offer already existed — it was just invisible where the audience is.
Main textEducational case-study framing with no reader benefitShort benefit-led text ending in one clear step. A worried homeowner needs a next action, not a reading assignment.
Trust proofNone visible in the adState-licensed mold remediators + 24/7 emergency service — facts already published on the business’s own website, moved into the ad where they can reassure.
Call to actionNo clear first step“Call Now”. Mold is an emergency purchase; the button should open the fastest route to help.
