What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • Labels are the whole message — naming rooms tells a reader what exists, but not why this campus is worth their membership or their Saturday.
  • The scale never appears — the ad does not mention the 135,000-square-foot facility, the 8 pickleball courts, or the 350-meter indoor track published on your website.
  • No benefit to the reader — nothing speaks to what a member, a parent, or a team actually gets from all that space.
  • No real first step — your website invites visitors to schedule a tour; the ad offers only a generic Send message button instead.

What the redesign does differently

  • A benefit leads, the list supports — the facilities now prove a promise instead of replacing one.
  • The scale is on the page — 135,000 square feet, 8 pickleball courts, a 350-meter track: the facts that make this campus different in Western New York.
  • Your tour becomes the call to action — the first step your site already publishes is now the ad’s destination.
  • Hours that sell — a 5:00am weekday opening answers the first question every serious member asks.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.
