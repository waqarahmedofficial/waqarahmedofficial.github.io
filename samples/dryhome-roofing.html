What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • Credentials carry the whole ad — licensing and longevity are important checks, but they do not give a homeowner a reason to act today.
  • No offer — the website publishes free roof inspections, yet the ad never puts that low-risk step in front of the audience.
  • No homeowner problem — storm damage may not be visible from the ground, but the ad does not name that worry or explain the inspection’s value.
  • Generic contact only — “contact us” does not tell the reader what will happen first.

What the redesign does differently

  • A real concern opens the ad — hidden wind and hail damage is the reason a homeowner needs an inspection.
  • The free inspection becomes the offer — it is already published on the website, so nothing new had to be invented.
  • The process reduces uncertainty — findings, next steps, and a comprehensive estimate explain what happens after the inspection.
  • Credentials move to support — licensed, bonded, and insured since 1988 remains available as reassurance instead of replacing the offer.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.
