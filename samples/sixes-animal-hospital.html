What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • Labels are the whole message — “full-service” and “locally owned” describe the hospital, but give a pet owner no reason to book a visit this week.
  • The welcome never appears — the ad does not say the hospital is now seeing new patients, the invitation published on your own homepage.
  • No service is named — wellness and dental care, surgery, diagnostics, and sick and emergency care all appear on your website; the ad names none of them.
  • No first step — your website offers an online appointment request; the ad offers only a Learn More button instead.

What the redesign does differently

  • The welcome leads — “now seeing new patients” is the one message a pet owner choosing a vet needs to hear first.
  • Services are named — wellness, dental, surgery, diagnostics, and emergency care, so a worried owner sees their situation answered.
  • The hours sell — open until 11:00pm on open days is a practical advantage most clinics do not offer, now stated in the ad.
  • Local ownership becomes a benefit — care for your pet like our own, in partnership with you, instead of a bare label.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
Lead messageService labels and location only: full-service, locally owned, in CantonYour own published invitation — now seeing new patients — as the headline. A practice that is welcoming patients should say so where the patients are.
Service offerNo service namedWellness and preventative care, dental, surgery, diagnostics, and sick and emergency care named in the ad, so the right owner recognises their need in seconds.
First stepNo step offered beyond a generic buttonYour own published online appointment request as the call to action. The step already existed on your website — it was just missing where the audience is.
Call to action“Learn More”“Request Appointment”. For a clinic taking new patients, the appointment request is the conversion — the button should say so.
