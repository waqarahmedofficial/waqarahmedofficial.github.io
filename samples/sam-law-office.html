What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • A label is not an offer — “family law and personal injury attorney” describes every competing firm in Chicagoland; it gives this reader no reason to choose yours.
  • Your free consultation is missing — your website advertises a free consultation; the ad never mentions it, although it is the lowest-risk first step a worried reader can take.
  • No benefit or proof — nothing speaks to the reader’s situation — a divorce, a custody fight, an injury — and your published record of over $1.5 million in injury settlements goes unused.
  • Call-only is the hardest step — your website also invites people to text the office; the ad offers only a phone call, the step hesitant readers most often postpone.

What the redesign does differently

  • The reader’s situation leads — divorce, custody, injury — named in the reader’s own terms instead of the firm’s job title.
  • Your free consultation becomes the offer — the step your website already advertises is now the reason to respond.
  • Your record does the persuading — over $1.5 million in injury settlements, from your own website, where a scrolling reader can actually see it.
  • An easier first step — call or text, both published on your website; texting lowers the barrier for someone not ready to talk.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
Lead messageA service label only: Chicagoland family law and personal injury attorneyA headline in the reader’s terms — divorce or injury, with a plan and a first step. Labels describe the firm; situations are what make a reader stop scrolling.
OfferNo offer of any kindYour own published free consultation, stated in the ad. A free first conversation removes the cost fear that keeps legal ads unanswered.
ProofNone in the adYour own published figure — over $1.5 million in injury settlements — on the creative. Readers choosing a lawyer look for a track record; yours was on your website but not in your ad.
Call to action“Call now” only“Call or text” for the free consultation, matching the options your website publishes. Two doors get more people through than one.
