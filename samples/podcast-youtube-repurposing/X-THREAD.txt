# X thread — Do Dogs Understand Fairness? The Treat Test That Split the Pack

# Post 1
Two dogs, side by side, doing the same simple job: give a paw. One gets a treat. The other gets nothing. What happens next is a question scientists actually tested. 🧵

# Post 2
In 2009, a team led by Friederike Range paid one dog for each paw and gave the other nothing at all. The unrewarded dogs gave the paw fewer times, then refused.

# Post 3
The control matters: dogs tested alone, with no partner, kept giving the paw far longer. The rewarded partner, not just the empty hand, ended the game.

# Post 4
Then the twist: both dogs paid, but one got sausage instead of bread. The bread dog kept working. A better treat for someone else was not enough to break the deal.

# Post 5
What this does not prove: dogs carry a human idea of fairness. What it shows is narrower — inequity aversion, a dislike of being left out entirely.
