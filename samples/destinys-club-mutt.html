What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • A service label as the whole headline — “Pet Boarding and Daycare” names the category, not a reason to stop scrolling. Every boarding facility in Colorado can say the same words.
  • No price anywhere — yet your own website publishes daycare at $26 a day. The clearest, most concrete reason to call is missing from the ad.
  • Nothing about the experience — your site sells spacious suites and a country setting with big outdoor play yards; the ad mentions none of it.
  • No clear first step — a label does not tell a pet owner what to do next or what a day costs.

What the redesign does differently

  • Your own $26 daycare rate, in the ad — the price already exists on your website; the redesign puts it where pet owners scroll.
  • A benefit headline, not a label — “A Country Day Out for Your Dog” sells the experience your site describes.
  • The differences, stated — big outdoor play yards, small matched play groups, and private suites with patios, all from your own site.
  • A direct booking step — “Book Now” with the price on the button line, instead of a bare category label.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
HeadlineThe service label itself: “Pet Boarding and Daycare”A benefit headline built on your country setting. A label describes a category; a benefit gives one facility a reason to be chosen.
Price / offerNo price or offer anywhere in the ad, although the website publishes daycare at $26 a day ($18 half day)Your own published daycare rate, placed in the ad. The price already existed — it was just invisible where the audience is.
Main textNo selling content beyond the labelShort benefit-led text using facts from your own site: big outdoor play yards, small groups matched by size and play style, private suites with patios.
Call to actionNo clear first step in the message“Book Now” with the rate beside it. For a daycare, booking the day is the conversion — the button should say so.
