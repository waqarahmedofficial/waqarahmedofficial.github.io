## FAQ

**1. What do you need from me to start?**
One sample of the data you work with (an Excel or CSV export is perfect) and a short note on the questions you want the dashboard to answer — for example "total sales by region each month." If your current report exists, send that too; I will match or improve its layout.

**2. Should I choose Excel or Google Sheets?**
Either works. Choose Excel if your files live on your computer or company network; choose Google Sheets if your team needs to view or edit the same file at the same time. The formulas and structure I build work the same in both, and I will advise you free before you order if you are unsure.

**3. My data is messy — is that a problem?**
No — messy data is the normal starting point. Standard includes light cleanup, and Premium includes a full Python cleaning pipeline: duplicates removed, casing fixed, invalid or missing e-mails flagged (never silently deleted), plus a written report of every change made.

**4. Will the dashboard really update itself when I add new data?**
Yes. Every KPI, summary and chart is a live formula over your raw data — nothing is typed in by hand. Add new rows, refresh, and the numbers rebuild. The documentation page shows you exactly where to paste new data so nothing breaks.

**5. Is my business data confidential?**
Completely. Your files are used only for your project, never shared, and never shown in my portfolio. Any samples you see in my gigs use fictional data I created myself. If your company requires it, I am happy to work with anonymised or partial data first.
