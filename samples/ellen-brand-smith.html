What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • The copy is a business card — a name, a title, and phone numbers tell a scrolling buyer or seller nothing about what Ellen sells or why she is the one to call.
  • No specialty is named — the brokerage’s recognised expertise in land, homes with acreage, and hunting, fishing, and timber property never appears.
  • No property and no market — no listing, no acreage, no area: a Madison-area land buyer scrolling past sees nothing that speaks to what they are looking for.
  • No first step beyond a cold phone call — the brokerage website offers current listings and listing alerts online; the ad offers neither, so an interested reader must do all the work themselves.

What the redesign does differently

  • A market leads, not a name — the copy speaks first to people buying or selling Mississippi land, the audience the ad is paying to reach.
  • The specialty is named — homes with acreage, hunting, fishing and timber land, and raw land, all from the brokerage’s own website.
  • The brokerage’s standing backs the agent — serving the Southeast since 2003 and a Best Brokerage listing by The Land Report give a stranger a reason to trust the call.
  • A first step short of a phone call — listing alerts let an interested buyer start a relationship today, instead of having to dial a number cold.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
Lead messageAn agent’s name, title, and phone numbers onlyA buyer-and-seller message about Mississippi land, with the agent and brokerage named as the specialists behind it. Contact details persuade after the reader cares; they cannot open the conversation alone.
SpecialtyNo property type, market, or expertise namedThe brokerage’s own specialties — land, homes with acreage, hunting, fishing and timber property — named in the ad, so the right buyer or seller recognises themselves in seconds.
First stepA phone number, with no reason or softer step offeredYour brokerage’s own published listing alerts and current listings as the first step. The step already existed on the website — it was just missing where the audience is.
Call to actionCall a number, cold“View Listings”, backed by listing alerts. A reader who will not phone a stranger today will still sign up for alerts — and that is how the relationship starts.
