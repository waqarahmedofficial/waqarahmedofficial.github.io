What a scrolling cat owner sees (and misses)

  • An announcement is not a reason — every clinic can say it welcomes new patients; the headline gives a cat owner no reason to choose this one.
  • The real differentiator is buried — “cats only” is the whole point of the clinic, but the ad never explains what that buys the cat: no dogs, no barking, vets who specialise in felines.
  • 32+ years never appears — your website leads with more than three decades of feline-exclusive care and private ownership; the ad carries none of that trust.
  • No service named — routine exams, dental care, surgery, boarding and emergency care are all on your website; a new owner with a specific need sees none of them.

What the redesign does differently

  • The differentiator leads — cats-only care, explained in terms of what it means for the cat: a bark-free clinic where felines are the only patients.
  • Trust is quantified — 32+ years of feline-exclusive care and private ownership, both from your own website, replace a bare announcement.
  • Services are named — exams, dental, surgery, boarding and emergency care, plus both locations, so a new owner sees their need covered.
  • A concrete step — your published phone number turns “welcoming” into an actual booking route.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.
