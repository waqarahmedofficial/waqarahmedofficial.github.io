What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • The headline is just a label and a city — a reader learns a medspa exists in New York, but not one treatment it offers or one reason to choose it.
  • No treatment is named — the copy stays vague, although your website offers specific, searchable treatments: Botox and Dysport, dermal fillers, laser treatments, body contouring, microneedling, and PRP.
  • No benefit to the reader — nothing speaks to what the client actually wants: skin that looks refreshed, with a plan built for them by licensed staff.
  • No concrete first step — your website has a clear “Book Now” step for both locations; the ad ends at “Learn more” instead.

What the redesign does differently

  • A client benefit leads — looking refreshed, instead of a label and a city.
  • Real treatments are named — Botox and Dysport, fillers, laser, body contouring, microneedling, and PRP, all from your own website, so a searching client recognises their treatment instantly.
  • Your Book Now step becomes the call to action — the first step your site already publishes is now the ad’s destination.
  • Trust facts on the creative — licensed and experienced staff, FDA-approved treatments, and an individualized plan: the reassurances a first-time client checks before booking.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.
