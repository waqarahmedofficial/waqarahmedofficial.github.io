What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • A service list is not an ad — every plumber in Ohio offers the same four services; a list gives no reason to choose Parsons.
  • No offer — no estimate, no guarantee, no first-visit reason of any kind is mentioned.
  • No benefit or outcome — the copy says what the company does, never what the homeowner gets.
  • Almost two years on the same copy — an ad running since December 2023 with list-only text is easy for scrollers to become blind to.

What the redesign does differently

  • An outcome headline — the full service range becomes one memorable promise instead of a bullet list.
  • Services become proof, not the message — they support the headline instead of carrying the whole ad.
  • A clear offer position — ready for a real first-step offer (placeholder in this sample).
  • Creative with a message on it — an original design a homeowner understands in one second.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
HeadlineNone — the service list is the whole adAn outcome headline that turns the range of services into a single promise. A list informs; a promise persuades.
Main textRemodeling, sewer and water lines, water heaters, drain cleaning — and a phone numberBenefit-led text written to the homeowner, with the services as supporting proof and one clear next step.
OfferNo offer of any kindA clear offer position (placeholder here — your real offer goes in). The first step has to feel easy and safe to take.
Call to action“Book now” under copy that gives no reason to bookThe same direct button, now earned by a headline and offer that make booking feel worthwhile.
CreativeGeneric image with no text or brandingOriginal design in a consistent company colour, headline on the image itself.
