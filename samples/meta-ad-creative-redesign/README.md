# Meta Ad Creative Redesign — Work Sample

By Waqar Ahmed — Spreadsheet & Data Specialist (7 years) · Portfolio: https://waqarahmedofficial.github.io

## What this sample shows

A redesign concept for a real, long-running Facebook/Instagram ad (Coastal Dental Group — the original ad had been running since March 2022). The original creative still carried stale "just in time for summer" copy in November, a generic stock-style image and no offer — even though the business's own website leads with a $79 new-patient special. The redesign leads with a season-proof benefit headline, brings the business's own $79 new-patient special into the ad where patients can see it, and replaces "Learn more" with a direct booking step. `pitch.md` explains every change and the reason for it.

## Files in this folder

- `sample.html` — the redesigned ad page (before/after reasoning built into the page)
- `pitch.md` — the change-by-change explanation written for the business

## Verification

Every change in the redesign is documented against the original ad and the business's own published offer in `pitch.md` — nothing in the new creative invents a price, claim or offer the business does not already make.

This is a sample built on fictional/practice data to demonstrate the service. Client work is delivered to the same standard.
