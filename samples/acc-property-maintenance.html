What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • A list is not a message — five service names tell a property manager what you do, but not why it is easier or safer to hand it all to you.
  • The strongest fact is missing — your website’s real story is one integrated company covering all of it, in the Mid-Atlantic since 2003; the ad never says it.
  • No client benefit — nothing about one call, one contract, or one accountable team replacing a patchwork of vendors.
  • No first step — the reader is given no action to take and no reason to take it now.

What the redesign does differently

  • The list becomes an argument — the same services, reframed as one integrated team under one call, which is the actual reason to choose ACC.
  • Your track record leads — serving the Mid-Atlantic since 2003, from your own website, now on the creative.
  • A direct first step — “Get in touch about your property” replaces a bare list with an action.
  • An offer slot, ready — the highlighted space is held for whatever offer you are currently running, the moment you want it in the ad.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
MessageA list of five service names, nothing moreA single benefit claim: one call covers every property service. Lists inform; claims persuade — and the claim here is simply the list, connected.
Proof / positioningNone in the adYour own published position — integrated property maintenance in the Mid-Atlantic since 2003 — stated on the creative. Longevity and integration are the two facts a property manager weighs first.
First stepNo step offered in the adA direct “get in touch about your property” step, with the offer slot held ready for your current offer. An ad without a next step is a sign, not a salesperson.
Call to action“Learn more”“Contact Us”. For a contract service, starting the conversation is the conversion — the button should say so.
