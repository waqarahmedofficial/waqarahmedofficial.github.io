What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • Credentials are the whole message — veteran-owned, honest, fair, on time: every competitor claims the same, and none of it tells a homeowner with no heat what happens next.
  • No problem is named — the ad never mentions the emergencies the website itself leads with: no heat or AC, water leaks and clogs, chimney and fireplace concerns.
  • No first step — the website lets a homeowner schedule service online in less than a minute; the ad offers no way to book anything at all.
  • “Like Page” spends the click on a follow — liking a page does not get a furnace fixed, so the one action the ad asks for cannot become a service enquiry.

What the redesign does differently

  • The homeowner’s problem leads — no heat, a leak, a chimney concern: the situations your website itself is organised around, named in the first line.
  • All four trades are named — HVAC, plumbing, chimney and fireplace, so a reader with any of those problems recognises the ad as theirs.
  • Credentials become proof, not the pitch — licensed & insured, background-checked technicians, and since 1995 now support a booking decision instead of replacing one.
  • A first step that books work — online scheduling in under a minute replaces a page like, so the click can actually become a job.
