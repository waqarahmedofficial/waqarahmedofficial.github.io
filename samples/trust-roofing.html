What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • Every roofer claims trust — “you can trust us” is the one sentence every competitor can also print, so it separates you from no one.
  • Your strongest offer is missing — your website offers a free roof inspection (“First one on us!”), and the ad never mentions an inspection at all.
  • Your best terms are missing too — no deposit on projects under $30,000 and a 10-year workmanship warranty are exactly the facts that make a homeowner pick up the phone, and neither appears.
  • No first step — the reader is given a feeling, not an action: nothing to book, claim or start.

What the redesign does differently

  • Your free inspection leads the ad — it is the easiest first step a homeowner can take, and it was already on your website waiting to be used.
  • Trust is shown, not claimed — no deposit under $30,000 and a 10-year workmanship warranty prove the headline instead of just printing it.
  • Risk removed in one read — free to check, nothing to pay upfront, payment on completion: three objections answered before the click.
  • A named first step — your site says it takes about 30 seconds to start; the ad now says so too.
  • A direct booking button — “Book Now” instead of “Learn more”.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
Headline / copy angleA generic trust claim: “The Local Roofing Company You Can Trust”The free inspection as the headline. A claim asks to be believed; an offer asks to be taken — and it demonstrates trust instead of asserting it.
OfferNo offer and no inspection hook anywhere in the adThe business’s own published offers: free first inspection and no deposit on projects under $30,000. Both already existed on the website — they were just invisible where the audience is.
Main textA slogan with nothing underneath itBenefit-led text that answers the three questions a homeowner actually has: what does it cost to check (nothing), what do I pay upfront (nothing under $30K), and who stands behind it (a 10-year workmanship warranty).
Call to action“Learn more”“Book Now” for the free inspection. A free, named first step converts better than an invitation to browse.
CreativeGeneric roof/crew imageryOriginal inspection-checklist design in a consistent company colour, headline on the image. The picture now says “free inspection” before a word is read.
