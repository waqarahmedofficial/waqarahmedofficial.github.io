What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • A credential is the headline — experience matters, but it does not explain how the team will present, market, or guide a particular home sale.
  • No reader benefit — a seller is left to guess what professional photography, virtual tours, and online marketing could do for their listing.
  • No audience is named — the profile serves buyers and sellers across several Long Island needs, yet the ad does not help one clear audience recognise itself.
  • Call-only first step — the profile also publishes an email route, but the ad makes a phone call the only way forward.

What the redesign does differently

  • One clear audience leads — Long Island sellers, especially in central Suffolk County, can immediately see that the ad is for them.
  • Experience becomes a visible service — photography, virtual tours, drone footage when appropriate, and online marketing show how the team works.
  • The benefit is presentation and reach — the copy explains what those marketing tools are meant to accomplish without promising a price or result.
  • Email joins the call — readers who are not ready to telephone can still take a real first step.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.
