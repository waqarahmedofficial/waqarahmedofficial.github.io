# Income & Expense Trackers — Work Sample

By Waqar Ahmed — Spreadsheet & Data Specialist (7 years) · Portfolio: https://waqarahmedofficial.github.io

## What this sample shows

A complete freelancer income & invoice tracker workbook, sheet by sheet: `Dashboard` (live totals — invoiced, received, outstanding, overdue, expenses, net income), `README` (the workbook's own built-in start guide), `Settings` (currency, year, monthly income goal), `Clients`, `Invoices` (status and balance due per invoice, with partial-payment handling), `Payments` and `Expenses`. Log a payment and the balances and dashboard update automatically. The product write-up, including its pre-publication checks, is in `LISTING_DRAFT_Product1_Freelancer_Tracker.md`.

The original sample is an Excel workbook; each sheet is exported here as CSV so it can be viewed directly on GitHub. Cells that hold live formulas in the workbook are exported as their formula text, so the calculation logic is visible; all other cells show their values.
