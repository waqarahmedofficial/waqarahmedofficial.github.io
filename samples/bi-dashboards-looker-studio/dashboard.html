What the exceptions mean

Every order whose settlement deviates from the fee schedule is listed below, ranked by money impact. Positive impacts (duplicate payouts) are money the platforms can reclaim — a risk, not a gain. Negative impacts are money the store was owed and never received.

All exceptions, ranked by impact

Source: exceptions

Insight. Five orders were never paid at all — $294.45 simply missing from settlement, led by ORD-1106 (−$87.08). Two orders were paid twice (+$160.18): that money is a clawback risk, not profit — the platforms can reclaim it. Fee overcharges are small ($13.67 across 4 orders) but prove the platforms' arithmetic cannot be taken on trust.
