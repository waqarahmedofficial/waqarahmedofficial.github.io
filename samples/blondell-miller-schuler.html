What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • The headline is interchangeable — a free evaluation is offered by every injury firm in the feed, so on its own it is no reason to stop, tap, or remember this firm.
  • The firm’s best fact never appears — your website promises the first appointment is with Mark Miller or Todd Schuler personally, not a paralegal; the ad never says it.
  • “No fees unless we win” is missing — the website leads with it; the ad leaves the reader’s biggest worry — what this will cost — unanswered.
  • Generic credentials, no benefit — the copy describes the firm in the same service language every competitor uses, without saying what changes for the injured person reading it.

What the redesign does differently

  • A distinct promise leads — “no fees unless we win” answers the reader’s first fear before she reads another word.
  • The attorney meeting becomes the hook — your published difference, a first appointment with the attorney himself rather than a paralegal, is something competitors in the feed do not say.
  • Experience is stated as a number — 50+ years of combined courtroom experience, from your own website, instead of generic credential language.
  • The evaluation gets a verb — “Get Evaluation” tells the reader exactly what the tap starts, with an immediate evaluation and same-day start behind it.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.
