What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • The headline is just the name — a reader who has never visited learns nothing about what is served or why to come.
  • No dish is named — for a cafe and pie shop, the ad never mentions pie, breakfast, or comfort food at all, although the website calls the cafe “The Comfort Food Specialists”.
  • No offer — your website runs a real one — Pie Happy Hour, weekdays 2–5pm, $1 off any pie slice — and the ad’s reader never hears of it.
  • Directions are the whole step — “Get directions” asks for a trip before the ad has given a single reason to make one.

What the redesign does differently

  • A named dish leads — pie, the thing the shop is named for, is finally the subject of the ad.
  • A real offer with a time window — Pie Happy Hour gives nearby readers a reason to come this week, not someday.
  • A loyalty hook readers remember — the returned-tin credit and Double Tin Tuesdays turn one visit into a habit.
  • Directions now have a reason — the same button works harder because the offer above it earns the trip.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
HeadlineThe business name onlyYour published offer as the headline — Pie Happy Hour, $1 off any slice. A name tells a new reader nothing; an offer with a time window gives them a plan.
Food focusGeneric nostalgia; no dish namedPie named first, with your site’s own positioning — The Comfort Food Specialists. People decide on food, not feelings; the ad should show them the food.
OfferNone in the adYour own published Pie Happy Hour and pie-tin return credit, exactly as your website states them. The offer already existed — it was just missing where the audience is.
Call to action“Get directions” with no reason given first“Get Directions” kept — it is the right step for a local cafe — but now it follows an offer worth travelling for.
