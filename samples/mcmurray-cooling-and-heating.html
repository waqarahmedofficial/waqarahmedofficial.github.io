What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • A label is not an offer — “HVAC company” describes hundreds of firms; it gives a homeowner with a dead furnace no reason to call this one.
  • The address does the ad’s only work — a street address matters to someone already driving over, not to someone deciding whom to call.
  • No problem is named — HVAC ads get answered in the moment something breaks: no heat, no cooling, a system to replace. The ad speaks to none of those moments.
  • Your scope stays invisible — your website covers furnaces, air conditioning, heat pumps, hydronic heating, and indoor air quality for homes and businesses; the ad shows none of it.

What the redesign does differently

  • The breakdown moment leads — “No heat? No cooling?” is the exact moment a homeowner reaches for the phone; the ad now meets them in it.
  • Your real scope is the offer — furnaces, air conditioning, heat pumps, and hydronic heating, from your own website, replace the bare label.
  • Family-owned becomes a trust line, not the whole message — it supports the offer instead of substituting for one.
  • The call is given a job — “tell us what your system is doing” tells the reader exactly what happens when they call, which a bare “Call now” never did.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
Lead messageA service label plus a street addressA headline in the homeowner’s moment — no heat, no cooling — with the firm as the answer. HVAC work is bought at breakdowns, not from labels.
Service offerNo service named beyond the categoryYour own published scope — furnaces, air conditioning, heat pumps, hydronic heating, indoor air quality, for homes and businesses. The right reader now recognises their own problem in the ad.
Trust line“Family-owned” carried the whole messageFamily-owned kept, but as the trust line under a real offer. Ownership reassures after the reader cares; it cannot make them care alone.
Call to action“Call now”, with no stated purpose“Call now” with a stated job — request service, describe the symptom. For a service company, the service call is the conversion — the ad should frame it that way.
