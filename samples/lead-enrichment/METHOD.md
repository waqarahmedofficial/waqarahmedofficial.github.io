# B2B Lead Enrichment — How the Service Works

**Done-for-you prospect data for agencies and B2B teams: ICP-matched, multi-source enriched, verified, and delivered ready to use in Google Sheets, Excel, or your CRM.**

You tell us exactly who your ideal customer is. We build the list — company by company, contact by contact — enrich it from multiple data sources, verify every email, and add a personalisation line for each lead, so your outreach team can start sending on day one.

---

## The Process

**1. ICP definition (in writing, before we build anything)**
You share your Ideal Customer Profile: industries, company size, countries/cities, job titles and seniority, technologies used, and anything to exclude (competitors, existing customers, wrong-fit segments). We confirm the targeting back to you in writing, so there are no surprises in the delivered file.

**2. Multi-source waterfall enrichment**
No single database has complete coverage, so we work waterfall-style: each record passes through multiple public and licensed data sources in sequence until every field is filled. A typical record includes:

- Company: name, website, industry, employee range, location, LinkedIn page
- Contact: full name, job title, seniority, department, LinkedIn profile
- Contactability: business email, plus phone where the tier includes it
- Signals: hiring, funding, technology, or growth indicators where publicly available

**3. Verification**
Every email goes through a multi-step validity check — syntax and domain checks, mail-server verification, and catch-all risk flagging — before it enters your file. Duplicates are removed, and the list is suppressed against any existing customer/prospect lists you give us, so you never pay for leads you already have.

**4. AI personalisation fields**
Each lead includes a short, human-reviewed personalisation line written from real public information about that company (their niche, services, or a specific claim on their site) — ready to drop into your cold email or LinkedIn opener. No generic "I saw your profile" filler.

**5. Delivery in your format**
A clean, ready-to-use file: Google Sheets or Excel, or a CRM-ready CSV mapped to HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Instantly, Smartlead, or Apollo field formats — just tell us which one you use.

## Validity & Replacement Guarantee

- We target **95%+ valid emails** in every delivered batch.
- If any email **hard-bounces within 14 days** of delivery, we **replace it free** — no questions, no forms.
- Catch-all and risky addresses are flagged in the file, never hidden, so you can segment them as you wish.
