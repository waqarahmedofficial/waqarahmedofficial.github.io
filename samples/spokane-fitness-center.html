What a scrolling reader sees (and misses)

  • Seasonal copy, wrong season — a “summer’s coming” message in November tells members and prospects alike that nobody is watching the ads.
  • No membership price or joining offer — fitness buyers compare on price and first step; the ad offers neither.
  • The Kid Zone is buried as a slogan — childcare is the single strongest reason a parent chooses one gym over another, and it is treated as a tagline.
  • “Book now” with nothing to book — no trial, tour or first step is named anywhere in the ad.

What the redesign does differently

  • The Kid Zone becomes the headline — the strongest parent-specific benefit, promoted from tagline to whole message.
  • Year-round copy — nothing in the redesign expires with a season.
  • A clear joining-offer position — ready for a real membership offer or trial (placeholder in this sample).
  • A named first step — the “Book Now” button now points at something concrete.

What we changed — and why

Every change below is about what a reader can see in the ad itself: the words, the image, and the button. Nothing here assumes anything about your results — only about how the ad reads.

ElementBeforeAfter — and the reason
Headline / copy angle“Summer’s Coming” seasonal copy, still live in NovemberA year-round benefit headline built on the Kid Zone. Seasonal copy has an expiry date; a parent’s childcare problem does not.
